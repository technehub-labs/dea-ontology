# Charter — Enterprise Ontology

> **Status:** placeholder. Full prose lands in Phase 1 of [CR-EO-01](./change-requests/CR-EO-01.md).

This document is the canonical grounding of **Enterprise Ontology (EO)** within the OpenDEA federation. It defines EO's core meaning, scope, and the boundary with its sibling discipline (Enterprise Semantic Architecture).

## Sections (Phase 1)

1. **Core meaning** — what EO is and is not.
2. **Position in OpenDEA** — placement in the layered model; the relationship to `dea-catalog-ontologies`.
3. **Scope and non-scope** — explicit enumeration of in-scope vs out-of-scope concerns.
4. **Relationship to sibling disciplines** — ESA, AR, CM-001, CR-8.
5. **Authority boundaries** — what this repo owns vs defers.
6. **Reversibility** — how an ontology can be deprecated, replaced, or migrated.
7. **Naming taste** — vocabulary discipline (the user has rejected archaic CMMI-era vocabulary; prefers digital-native terms).
8. **Upper-ontology stance** — EO does NOT mandate a single upper ontology; sector ontologies declare their choice.

## Phase 1 deliverable targets

- Full prose for all eight sections.
- Cross-references to CR-EO-01, OpenDEAM, ECF, Concepts Model, Metamodel, sibling ESA repo, and `dea-catalog-ontologies`.
- An axiom-derivation table mapping every EO tenet to one or more ECF axiom-grid cells.
