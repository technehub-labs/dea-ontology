# Tenets — Enterprise Ontology

> **Status:** placeholder. Full prose lands in Phase 1 of [CR-EO-01](./change-requests/CR-EO-01.md).

EO tenets are **axiom-derived**, not invented. Every tenet traces to one or more cells of the ECF 7×7 axiom grid (`technehub-labs/dea-metaframework`), or is explicitly flagged as a sector-specific extension (deferred to CR-EO-02 onward).

## Phase 1 deliverable targets

- Three tenets, each with:
  - **Statement** — the tenet itself.
  - **Axiom source** — ECF cell(s) it derives from.
  - **Operational rule** — how the tenet becomes enforceable in day-to-day EO practice.
  - **Anti-pattern** — what violates the tenet.
- Cross-references to CR-EO-01, OpenDEAM, ECF, Concepts Model.

## Three placeholder tenets (final wording in Phase 1)

1. **Ontological commitments are explicit.** Every class, property, and restriction declares its ontological commitment; implicit assumptions are flagged for review.
2. **Upper-ontology choice is sector-specific, not federation-mandated.** Each sector ontology declares its upper-ontology choice in its sector-tenets; EO does not impose one.
3. **Ontology lifecycle is governed.** Versioning, deprecation, retirement, mapping-to-replacement, and archive policy are first-class concerns; ontologies are never silently evolved.
